So anything you make with Claude looks like Good Cup, not like a default AI page. Two minutes.

Please set up the Good Cup design look for me, then use it for everything I make for Good Cup.

1. The skill pack is a small zip file at this address: https://kirkvvisitacion-stack.github.io/studio/handoff/packs/goodcup-design-skill-pack.zip
2. If you are Claude Code: download and unzip it, then copy the folder goodcup-look into ~/.claude/skills/ (create that folder if it is missing). Check that ~/.claude/skills/goodcup-look/SKILL.md exists, and tell me the result.
3. If you are Cowork or claude.ai and cannot install skills yourself: tell me to open Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills, upload the zip and switch it on. Then wait for me to say it is done.
4. Read SKILL.md and checklist.md from the pack. Tell me what the look is in three short lines.
5. From now on, for every Good Cup post, deck, report, web page or video title: load the goodcup-look skill first, build with its tokens, run its checklist, and tell me which checks you fixed.
6. Use DM Sans where Good Sans is not available. Do not add fonts or photos I did not give you.
Download the pack

How to do it

  1. Step 1

    Download the pack

    Press the button under the prompt. You get one small zip file, about 6 KB. Keep it in your Downloads folder.

  2. Step 2

    Copy the prompt

    Press Copy the prompt at the top of this page. It is one block of text.

  3. Step 3

    Paste it into your Claude

    Open Claude Code, Cowork or claude.ai, start a new chat and paste. Claude Code installs the pack by itself. On claude.ai and Cowork, Claude will ask you to upload the zip: open Settings, then Capabilities, then Skills, upload the zip and switch it on.

  4. Step 4

    Make something

    Ask for a post, a deck or a one page report. Claude uses the Good Cup look and tells you which checks it fixed.

Bold headline, regular body, left aligned. DM Sans stands in for Good Sans on the web.

When to use it

  • Posts and carousels. Instagram slides, Stories and captions with real photos.
  • Decks. Team and partner slides in white and crema.
  • Reports and one pagers. Plain documents the team reads on a phone.
  • Web pieces. Landing pages and tool pages like this one.

What is not in the pack

  • The Good Sans font files. They are licensed, so they stay inside Good Cup. The pack uses DM Sans on the web, so pages come out close but not letter perfect. For print or slides, ask the team lead for the font files.
  • The photo library. Your Claude leaves a marked photo slot until you give it a real Good Cup photo.
  • The sticker and bag picture method, and the checker script the design owner runs. The pack carries the same rules as a 25 line checklist instead.
  • Claude cannot install a skill on claude.ai for you. That one step, the upload in Settings, is yours.
  • The company account may already have a skill called good-cup-design. It is a separate, older look. If both are on, tell Claude which one to use.

Check it worked

Ask your Claude: "Which skills do you have for Good Cup?" It should name goodcup-look. A page it makes should have a white ground, sentence case headlines, and no gradients, emoji or all caps labels.
