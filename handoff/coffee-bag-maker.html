Make a 3D Coffee Bag handoff

Use our exact studio bags in your own Claude work. No Blender needed.

Make a 3D Coffee Bag Jump to the prompt

About 5 minutes. Five steps below.

Why this handoff exists

The studio bags were built in Blender, and only Kirk has Blender. Make a 3D Coffee Bag puts your sticker on the exact same bag in your browser. You get bag pictures and a 3D file, and your own Claude can place them in any layout, so nobody waits for Kirk.

Copy this prompt

You are helping me, a Good Cup teammate, make a studio coffee bag with my own sticker on it. I do not use Blender and I do not need it.

THE TOOL
Make a 3D Coffee Bag is a web page that runs in the browser:
https://kirkvvisitacion-stack.github.io/goodcup-bagmaker/

WHAT I GIVE YOU
My sticker, as a print PDF from Canva (keep the Canva file name, it holds the control number and the coffee name), or as a square PNG that is exactly 1800 x 1800 pixels.

DO THIS
1. Make a 3D Coffee Bag. If you cannot use a browser yourself, tell me each click and wait for me.
2. Drop my sticker into the first box. Read the control number and coffee name it found. Fix them if they are wrong, because they become the file names.
3. Check the bag it picked. Bag types: 200 g, 500 g, 1 kg, 100 g Rare, 50 g Rare. Side colours: Philippine coffee is yellow, seasonal coffee from another country is white, Espresso, AYR and Daily Driver are brown, Rare is black. If the pick looks wrong, ask me before you go on.
4. Press Make the bag. The first bag takes about a minute.
5. Compare the front picture with my sticker, word by word. If any word differs, stop and tell me. Tick the check box only when every word matches.
6. Download the folder (a zip) and unzip it. It holds 01 Front, 02 Angled, 03 45 Degrees and 04 Back as PNG pictures, plus 06 Extras (the 3D model as a GLB, the front cutout, the shadow layer) and 07 the sticker PDF.

IF I ASK FOR A LAYOUT
When I ask for a post, slide, menu, poster or any layout, place the downloaded bag PNG (or the GLB for 3D) into it. These rules always apply:
- Never change the sticker artwork. No retyping, recolouring, cropping, redrawing, upscaling or AI redraw. A model must never draw printed words.
- Never grade, brighten or recolour the bag picture. Only move, scale and rotate the whole bag, and keep its shadow layer with it.
- Keep the sticker centred on the bag as delivered. Do not squash or stretch the bag.
- Use only the exact studio bag from the download, never a different bag drawn for the layout.
- When the layout is done, compare it with the original bag PNG. Zoom into the sticker and read every word again, and check the bag colours match. Tell me what you checked.

IF I NEED SOMETHING NEW
If I need a bag shape or size that is not in the list above, stop and tell me to ask Kirk. A new bag shape needs Blender, which only Kirk has.

WHERE FINISHED BAGS GO
On the Good Cup NAS: 01 New Bags - Studio Look Library / 18 New Sticker Bags - Inbox / 2 Finished bags (pick up here)
I upload the whole unzipped folder there myself. Tell me never to move, rename or delete anything else on the NAS.

Paste it into Claude Code, Cowork or claude.ai, then attach your sticker.

What to do

  1. Step 1

    Copy the prompt

    Press the Copy button above. It copies the whole prompt in one tap.

  2. Step 2

    Paste it into your Claude

    Open Claude Code, Cowork or claude.ai. Paste the prompt, then attach your sticker: the print PDF from Canva, or a square 1800 x 1800 PNG.

  3. Step 3

    Let Claude run the Make a 3D Coffee Bag

    Claude opens the page, drops in your sticker, checks the bag and sides, and downloads the zip. If it cannot click for you, it tells you each click. You only read the words and say yes.

  4. Step 4

    Ask for your layout

    Tell Claude what you are making: a post, a slide, a menu, a poster. Claude Design or any Claude places the bag PNG into it and keeps the rules in the prompt.

  5. Step 5

    Hand the folder in

    Upload the unzipped folder to the NAS inbox below. Then send the team message in the team chat.
