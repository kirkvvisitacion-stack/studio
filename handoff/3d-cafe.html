Use the 3D of the cafe in your Claude

This is for teammates who want to use the 3D of the Manila cafe (the whole cafe, the Slow Bar and the floor plan) inside their own Claude work. You can place a drink or a sign in the cafe, plan a layout, or make visuals. You do not need Blender or Tailscale. You paste one prompt and your Claude does the work.

How to do it

  1. Step 1

    Get the files

    Press Get the handoff files below. It opens the Good Cup NAS share. The whole package is about 512 MB, so download it on the office network, not on mobile data. The 3D Model Files folder alone is about 210 MB if you only need the models.

  2. Step 2

    Keep them on your computer

    Unzip the download and put the folder somewhere easy, like Documents. Do not open the files from the share each time. Copy them to your computer first.

  3. Step 3

    Open your own Claude

    Use Claude Code, Cowork or claude.ai. Point it at the folder (in Claude Code or Cowork, open the folder as your project; in claude.ai, attach the GLB file).

  4. Step 4

    Paste the prompt and fill one gap

    Press Copy the prompt, paste it into Claude, then replace the part in square brackets with what you want placed or made.

  5. Step 5

    Look at the screenshots

    Claude saves pictures in a new folder called my 3D work. Ask for changes in plain words, like "move it 50 cm to the left".

The prompt for your Claude

Press the button, then paste it into Claude. Fill the one part in square brackets, what you want placed or made.

I want to use the 3D model of the Good Cup Manila cafe in my work. The model files are in the Good Cup NAS handoff share: https://ug.link/goodcup/filemgr/share-download/?id=5bec6ab99acb40f99cdbc5a2ad78ec5a . If you cannot open that link, tell me, and I will download the folder and give you its path.

Files in that folder:
- 01 Whole Cafe.glb: the whole cafe with the roof off, real size in metres. Use this one by default.
- 04 Slow Bar.glb: the Slow Bar room on its own.
- 05 Cafe Floor Plan.glb: plain walls, doors and counters, good for measuring and layouts.
(02 Whole Cafe.usdz and 03 Whole Cafe.blend are for iPhone and Blender. Ignore them.)

Rules:
1. Never edit, move, rename or delete the original files. Work on copies only.
2. Save everything you make in a NEW folder next to them called "my 3D work", with a short name for each output.
3. Use plain, short words when you explain things to me.

What to do:
1. Load the GLB with three.js (a small local HTML page is fine, use a local web server if the browser blocks the file). Inspect the model: tell me the overall size in metres, the names of the main objects and rooms, and where the floor is.
2. Take a top-view screenshot and a corner-view screenshot so I can see you loaded it correctly. Save them in "my 3D work".
3. Then do this for me: [DESCRIBE WHAT YOU WANT, for example: "Put a box labeled 'Cold brew stand' on the counter nearest the front door, 60 cm wide, and show me a screenshot from the entrance." or "Show a 1.2 m tall sign next to the window and label it 'Menu board'."]. Add the labeled object as a separate item on top of the model. Name the spot in words (for example "left end of the Slow Bar counter") and tell me the coordinates you used.
4. Take screenshots from two or three angles, save them, and tell me where they are.
5. If something does not look right, say what you see and fix it on your copy. Do not guess sizes: ask me.

Check it worked. Claude tells you the size of the cafe in metres and gives you two screenshots (top view and corner view) in a new folder. If you see the cafe in them, it worked.
