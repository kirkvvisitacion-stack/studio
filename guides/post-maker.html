Put words on your photos.

Make an Instagram carousel and Story in the Good Cup look, right in the browser.

Open the post maker

About 10 minutes the first time. Five steps below.

How to use it

  1. Step 1

    Load the fonts, once

    The Good Cup font is on the NAS. Download all 16 files, then press Choose font files in the post maker and pick them. You do this once on each computer. The green line Good Cup fonts ready means you are set.

  2. Step 2

    Add your photos

    Press Add photos and pick one photo per slide, in posting order. Drag a photo to choose which part shows. Carousel and Story are framed separately, so check both.

  3. Step 3

    Write the words

    Type in the words box. To make some words bold or italic, select them first, then press the button. Drag the words to move them, and keep them off busy parts of the photo.

  4. Step 4

    Read every slide and download

    Name the post, paste the caption, then read each slide in Carousel and Story. Check that every date matches its day. Tick the box and press Download the post.

  5. Step 5

    Save it to the NAS

    Unzip the download and upload the whole folder to the inbox below. Then copy the team message from the page and send it in the team chat.

Before you start

  • Your photos, one per slide
  • The Good Sans font files from the NAS (first time only)
  • Chrome or Safari on a computer or phone
  • Your NAS login, to hand in the folder

What you get

  • One zip with a Carousel folder (1080x1350) and a Story folder (1080x1920)
  • 00 Caption.txt with your caption
  • A .gcpost project file, so anyone can reopen and edit the post

Hand it in here

01_Final_Outputs / Photos / 00 Post Maker - Inbox

Upload only into that folder. Never move, rename or delete anything else on the NAS.

If something looks wrong

It says fonts are missing

Do step 1 again. On a computer that already has Good Sans installed, the fonts load by themselves.

My iPhone photo does not open

HEIC photos open in Safari. In other browsers, save the photo as a JPG first.

I want to fix a post later

Press Open project file and pick the .gcpost file from the post folder.

Someone else should finish it

Send them the .gcpost file. It keeps the photos, words and positions.

Iowan looks wrong on my laptop

Iowan comes with Mac and iPhone. On Windows or Android, use Good Sans.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere. Everything happens in your browser. Only the files you download leave it.
