About 5 minutes. Five steps below.

How to use it

  1. Step 1

    Get the sticker PDF

    In Canva, download the sticker as PDF Print. Keep the Canva file name, for example 26-112_Finca-de-Garces_PH-3x3.pdf. The page reads the control number and coffee name from it.

  2. Step 2

    Drop it into step 1

    Drag the PDF onto the box, or tap it to choose the file. Read the control number and the coffee name. Fix them if they are wrong, because they become the file names.

  3. Step 3

    Pick the bag and press Make

    The page picks the bag from your sticker. Check it: yellow sides for Philippine coffees, white for seasonal, brown for all year round, black for Rare. Then press Make the bag. The first bag takes a minute, after that about 5 seconds.

  4. Step 4

    Read every word

    Look at the front picture next to your sticker. Read each word on both. Tick the box only when they match.

  5. Step 5

    Save it to the NAS

    Press Download the folder, unzip it, and upload the whole folder to the inbox below. Then copy the team message from the page and send it in the team chat.

Before you start

  • The sticker as a print PDF from Canva (3x3, or 4x3 for 500 g and 1 kg)
  • Chrome or Safari on a computer
  • Your NAS login, to hand in the folder

What you get

  • 01 Front, 02 Angled, 03 45 Degrees, 04 Back (PNG pictures)

If something looks wrong

The page says the sticker needs a check

Read the note under the sticker. If it mentions a dark edge on a black bag, send the sticker and a screenshot to the team chat before you use the pictures.

The bag size I need is not listed

The page makes 200 g, 500 g and 1 kg (yellow, white, brown sides), 100 g Rare and 50 g Rare. Anything else, ask Kirk.

The first bag is slow

The first bag of each type downloads about 40 MB. Keep the tab open. The next ones take seconds.

A word on the bag looks different from the sticker

Do not tick the box. Send the sticker and a screenshot to the team chat.

There is no 360 video

Right, it is not on the page yet. Ask Kirk if you need one.
