The rules it follows

  • Sentence caseCapitals only where a sentence needs them.
  • Left alignedText lines up on the left, never centred in paragraphs.
  • One idea per pieceOne message per slide or section.
  • Real photos onlyNo stock look. A marked slot until you have the photo.
